Deposit Methods

Accepted deposit methods include PayID, Beem, cryptocurrency or gift card at my discretion.

Clients requiring a greater degree of discretion are encouraged to use cryptocurrency or an approved gift card.

Please ensure your preferred method is established and ready prior to confirming the booking.

PayID details are discreet and are not associated with my professional services. Details may be provided using an email address or separate mobile number that is not used for booking enquiries, providing additional separation between payment and correspondence.

Cash Balance

The remaining balance is payable in cash upon meeting. Please arrive with the full agreed amount available, including sufficient cash for any agreed extensions or additional charges.

ATM withdrawal limits can restrict the amount available at short notice, particularly for longer engagements. Please organise sufficient cash in advance and allow adequate time to visit a bank branch if required.

Payment Policy

All agreed booking fees are payable in full. Withholding, reversing, disputing or attempting to recover an agreed payment after a booking has taken place will result in active recovery of any amount owing.

Where appropriate, a matter may be pursued through civil proceedings and/or referred to the relevant authorities where the circumstances may constitute a criminal offence.

FAQ

Are deposits required?

Yes. A deposit is required to secure every booking.

Are deposits refundable?

Deposits are generally non-refundable where a client cancels or is unable to attend. Any alternative arrangement is entirely at my discretion.

How discreet are in-call arrangements?

Discretion is carefully considered when selecting in-call locations, particularly throughout regional and rural areas. Accommodation is selected with privacy, comfort and discreet access as priorities.

Will a regional tour be cancelled if demand is low?

Tour dates with confirmed pre-bookings are always honoured irrespective of overall enquiry volume. Dates without confirmed pre-bookings may be modified or cancelled.

Can early-morning appointments be arranged?

Yes. Dawn and early-morning appointments can be arranged with sufficient advance notice where availability permits.

Do you use social media?

I do not currently operate any official social media accounts. My website and listed contact details should be used for current availability and booking information.
