FAQ

Are deposits required?

Yes. A confirmation deposit is required to secure every booking.

Are deposits refundable?

Deposits are generally non-refundable where the client cancels or is unable to attend. Any alternative arrangement is entirely at my discretion.

How discreet are in-call locations?

Discretion is carefully considered when selecting accommodation and in-call locations, particularly throughout regional and rural areas. Exact details are provided once your booking is confirmed.

Will a regional tour be cancelled if demand is low?

Tour dates with confirmed pre-bookings are always honoured irrespective of overall enquiry volume. Dates without confirmed pre-bookings remain subject to modification.

Can I arrange an early-morning booking?

Yes. Dawn and early-morning appointments can be arranged with sufficient advance notice where availability permits.
